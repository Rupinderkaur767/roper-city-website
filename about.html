WELCOME TO ROPAR

The Heart of Rupnagar

Ropar, officially known as Rupnagar, is a historic city of Punjab situated along the Satluj River. The city is known for its archaeological heritage, religious places, cultural traditions and beautiful natural surroundings.

From ancient archaeological discoveries to peaceful riverside landscapes, Ropar offers visitors an opportunity to experience the rich heritage and vibrant culture of Punjab.

Ropar is an important destination for people interested in history, spirituality, nature and Punjabi culture. Its museums, historical sites, religious places and natural attractions make it a unique place to explore.

The city provides a beautiful combination of the past and present, where historical heritage and modern life exist together.
